# Password Reset

Customers can reset their password using the Forgot Password link.

Password reset emails expire after 30 minutes.

If the customer no longer has access to the registered email address,
identity verification is required before changing the email.

## How to reset your password
1. Go to the login page and click "Forgot Password".
2. Enter your registered email address.
3. Open the password reset link sent to your email (expires in 15 minutes).
4. Enter your new password (minimum 8 characters, 1 number, 1 special character).
